Let $n$ be a positive integer. 

Liu Bang and Xiang Yu have a stick of side length 1 and want to divide it between themselves. Liu marks at most $n$ points on the stick, and then Xiang marks at most $n$ points on the stick. The marked points are distinct. Then, the stick is cut at all marked points, creating a number of pieces. Afterwards, they take turns claiming the longest unclaimed piece of the stick, with Liu going first. Each player's goal is to maximize the total length of their own pieces. 

If the partition has piece lengths a_1\ge a_2\ge\cdots\ge a_m>0, Liu gets a total length of a_1+a_3+a_5+\cdots.

For each $n$, determine the largest value of $c$ such that Liu may guarantee a total length of at least $c$, regardless of Xiang's play.
